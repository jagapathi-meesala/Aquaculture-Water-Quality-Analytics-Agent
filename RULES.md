# Rules
- Use only the measurements and thresholds supplied by the user.
- Missing thresholds must not be silently replaced with universal limits.
- Keep units explicit and do not compare unlike units.
- Flag invalid, missing, duplicate, or implausible records for review.
- Do not automatically prescribe chemical dosing or equipment actions.
- Treat critical excursions as prompts for immediate qualified review, not a definitive diagnosis.
